## English

This release adds content pinning, remembers the clipboard panel size, and refreshes the app icons.

### Added

- Pin clipboard items to keep them at the top, with pin status and ordering preserved after restarting the app.
- Download updates in the background, with progress shown in the main panel and Settings.

### Improvements

- Remember the clipboard panel size and adapt the layout to narrower windows.
- Display formatted release notes in the update dialog.
- Refresh the desktop and tray icons, use the tray artwork in panel and Settings titles, and refine the main panel icon size.

## 简体中文

本次更新新增内容置顶、面板尺寸记忆，并更换了应用图标。

### 新增

- 支持置顶剪贴板内容，重启应用后保留置顶状态和排序。
- 支持后台下载更新，可在主面板和设置中查看下载进度。

### 改进

- 记住剪贴板面板尺寸，并优化窄窗口下的布局。
- 更新弹窗支持显示排版后的更新说明。
- 更换桌面和托盘图标，主面板及设置标题统一使用托盘图案，并调整主面板图标尺寸。
