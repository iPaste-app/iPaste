## English

- Reorganize the main toolbar and add keyboard-friendly quick search. Jump to a result in its original history or category without filtering the main list.
- Expand language support from 7 to 17 languages, including the tray menu and LAN sharing page.
- Add dedicated icons for Android, Windows, and Apple installers, e-books, and databases. Reduce the size of the bundled file icons without changing how large they appear.

## 简体中文

- 调整主面板工具栏，新增支持键盘操作的快速搜索。可直接跳转到历史记录或分类中的对应条目，不会筛掉主列表中的其他内容。
- 支持的语言从 7 种增加到 17 种，托盘菜单和局域网分享页也会随语言设置切换。
- 为 Android、Windows 和 Apple 安装包、电子书及数据库文件新增专用图标，并缩减内置文件图标的体积，保持显示尺寸不变。
